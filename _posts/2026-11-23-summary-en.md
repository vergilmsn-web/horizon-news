ServeTheHome has released a review of the Centec ZX530S-24S2CQ, a budget-friendly network switch featuring 24 SFP+ 10G ports and two 100G SFP28 ports. The device is powered by a Centec switch chip, positioning it as an affordable alternative in the high-speed switching market. This switch offers cost-effective 10G and 100G connectivity for users who need affordable high-speed networking without premium enterprise pricing. It expands options for enthusiasts and small-to-medium businesses looking to build high-performance networks on a budget. The ZX530S-24S2CQ combines 24 SFP+ ports for 10G connectivity with two SFP28 ports capable of 100G speeds, utilizing Centec silicon as its core processing component. This hybrid port configuration supports diverse bandwidth needs within a single compact chassis.

rss · ServeTheHome · Nov 22, 18:00

**Background**: SFP+ and SFP28 are compact optical transceiver form factors, with SFP+ typically supporting 10G speeds and SFP28 supporting 25G or higher rates, though the switch can configure SFP28 ports for 100G. Centec is a semiconductor company that designs Ethernet switch chips, such as the CTC8180, aimed at low-latency and edge computing applications. Budget switches using non-traditional or alternative silicon often appeal to homelab enthusiasts and cost-conscious IT departments seeking performance without the price tag of major vendors.

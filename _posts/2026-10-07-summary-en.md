## [AI Data Centers Should Track Energized Compute Over Gigawatts](https://www.eetimes.com/the-ai-boom-has-a-gigawatt-accounting-problem/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An EE Times article argues that the AI boom suffers from a 'gigawatt accounting problem' where raw power capacity is prioritized over actual utilization. It proposes 'energized compute' as the superior metric, citing significant delays in memory, networking, cooling, and power infrastructure that prevent immediate conversion of gigawatts to useful AI workloads. This analysis is critical for data center planning because it highlights that merely building power capacity does not equate to AI capability, as physical constraints in other supply chain components create bottlenecks.

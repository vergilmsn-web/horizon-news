## [DDR5 Memory Prices Surge Up to 485% Year-Over-Year](https://www.techpowerup.com/351689/memory-prices-break-a-record-with-nearly-500-surge-in-the-last-12-months) ⭐️ 7.5/10

DDR5 memory prices have surged between 372% and 485% year-over-year according to PCPartPicker data, with a 64GB DDR5-6000 kit rising from $222 to $1,272. DDR4 kits have also risen significantly, though less dramatically, by 120% to 177%. This unprecedented price spike impacts consumers, PC builders, and gamers looking to upgrade or build systems, potentially slowing hardware refresh cycles. It also raises costs for AI and data center infrastructure that relies heavily on high-capacity DRAM, potentially cascading into broader computing costs. The price increases vary by speed and capacity, with 2x32GB 5,600 MT/s kits seeing the steepest 485% rise, while 2x16GB 4,800 MT/s kits rose 372%. Eurozone data from ComputerBase shows an even steeper 345% year-over-year increase for August, suggesting the trend is global and may continue climbing.

rss · TechPowerUp News · Aug 18, 16:16

**Background**: DDR5 is the fifth generation of Double Data Rate SDRAM, standardized by JEDEC and launched commercially in Q4 2021, offering higher bandwidth, larger capacities, and lower voltage (1.1V vs DDR4's 1.2V) compared to its predecessor. MT/s (mega transfers per second) is the technically correct unit for measuring DDR memory speed, as it reflects actual data transfer rate rather than clock frequency. DDR5 memory is now the standard for modern desktop and laptop platforms, making its pricing directly relevant to virtually all new PC builds.

Othisis, a two-year-old Indian startup, has successfully demonstrated a fully 3D-printed, reusable cryogenic rocket engine produced using selective laser melting (SLM), marking the company's first working prototype of the engine. This milestone shows that additive manufacturing can produce not just simple metal parts but entire complex, high-performance cryogenic propulsion systems, potentially reducing manufacturing time and cost for rocket engines. It also positions India among a growing group of private space startups demonstrating cutting-edge propulsion capabilities outside of established government agencies. rss · Tom's Hardware · Aug 18, 10:00

**Background**: Selective Laser Melting (SLM) is a metal 3D printing technique in which a high-power laser selectively melts layers of metallic powder according to a digital CAD model, building up solid metal parts layer by layer — typically in slices 20–100 micrometers thick. Cryogenic rocket engines use propellants stored at extremely low temperatures, most commonly liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen, and are almost exclusively pump-fed to deliver propellant into the combustion chamber. These engines typically require complex internal cooling channels to handle extreme thermal conditions, making them ideal candidates for additive manufacturing. Reusable rocket engines, popularized by companies like SpaceX, are designed to survive multiple flights, refueling, and reignition cycles, dramatically lowering the cost of access to space.

## [Applied Materials Proposes Systems-to-Materials Co-Optimization Beyond Moore](https://semiwiki.com/semiconductor-manufacturers/372192-beyond-moore-co-optimizing-ai-from-systems-to-materials/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

At the OCP APAC Summit in Taiwan on August 11–12, 2026, Applied Materials' Subi Kengeri delivered a keynote framing AI as the semiconductor industry's largest inflection point and proposing co-optimization from systems to materials as a path beyond Moore's Law, with AI demand projected to drive the industry toward one trillion dollars in annual revenue. This keynote signals a strategic shift in how the semiconductor industry must innovate to sustain performance scaling in the AI era, as traditional transistor shrinking alone can no longer deliver the efficiency gains required for hyperscale AI workloads. The co-optimization approach matters because it involves every layer of the stack — from data center architecture down to new materials — affecting chip designers, equipment makers, and AI infrastructure providers alike. The concept builds on System Technology Co-Optimization (STCO), a holistic design methodology that simultaneously optimizes multiple layers of a computing system for performance, power, area, and cost (PPAC). Applied Materials extends this idea further by elevating materials science — including emerging players like Thintronics — to a first-class design variable rather than a background consideration.

rss · SemiWiki · Aug 18, 17:00

**Background**: Moore's Law, the decades-old observation that transistor density doubles roughly every two years, has driven semiconductor progress since the 1960s but is now slowing due to physical and economic limits at advanced nodes. 'Beyond Moore' refers to a collection of strategies — including 3D stacking, chiplets, advanced packaging, novel transistor architectures (like Gate-All-Around and CFET), and new materials — to continue delivering performance gains. The OCP (Open Compute Project) APAC Summit focuses on data center infrastructure challenges, making it a natural venue for Applied Materials to connect materials innovation with AI compute demands.

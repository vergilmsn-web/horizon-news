## [DRAM Memory Prices Surge Up to 485% Year-Over-Year, Hitting Record Highs](https://www.techpowerup.com/351689/memory-prices-break-a-record-with-nearly-500-surge-in-the-last-12-months) ⭐️ 7.5/10

According to PCPartPicker data cited by TechPowerUp, DDR5 memory kit prices have climbed between 372% and 485% compared to August of the previous year, while DDR4 kits have risen 120% to 177%. A 6,000 MT/s 64 GB DDR5 kit that previously cost $222 now sells for $1,272, and German tracker ComputerBase reports a 345% year-over-year increase in the Eurozone, with no signs of price relief. This unprecedented price spike directly impacts PC builders, gamers, and consumers upgrading hardware, making new builds significantly more expensive. It also signals serious supply-side constraints in the DRAM market, largely driven by AI and data center demand reallocating manufacturing capacity away from consumer products. PCPartPicker only tracks US pricing, so these figures may underestimate global trends; Eurozone data shows even steeper jumps. Speed ratings like 4,800 MT/s, 5,600 MT/s, and 6,000 MT/s refer to megatransfers per second — the number of data transfer operations per second — and higher-speed DDR5 kits have seen the most extreme price increases, suggesting tight supply of premium bins.

rss · TechPowerUp News · Aug 18, 16:16

**Background**: DRAM (Dynamic Random-Access Memory) is the main volatile memory used in computers, with each cell consisting of one transistor and one capacitor that must be periodically refreshed. DDR5 is the current generation of desktop memory, offering higher frequencies (up to 9.6 GT/s) and lower voltage (1.1V vs DDR4's 1.2V) compared to its predecessor DDR4. Speed is measured in MT/s (megatransfers per second), which indicates how many data operations the memory can perform per second and depends on compatibility with the CPU and motherboard.

Japanese chemical maker Ajinomoto has reportedly informed its mainland China customers that it will cut supply of ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film), a critical dielectric material used in advanced chip packaging, by 30%. The reduction is reportedly a response to Beijing's rare earth export curbs and has accelerated Chinese efforts to qualify domestic substitute materials. Ajinomoto controls approximately 95% of the global ABF market, making this a near-monopolistic choke point for advanced chip packaging. Qualifying a replacement dielectric in advanced packaging typically requires 12–18 months of validation with substrate makers and chip designers, meaning any disruption has long-lasting ripple effects.

## [DSP Pioneer Bede Liu Passes Away at 91](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=85136) ⭐️ 7.3/10

IEEE Spectrum published an obituary honoring Bede Liu, a founding figure of modern digital signal processing (DSP), who passed away in May 2025 at the age of 91. Liu joined Princeton University in 1962 and co-authored the influential 1976 textbook 'Digital Signal Processing: Theory, Design, and Implementation' with his former student Abe Peled, which became a standard reference for engineers and helped define the DSP profession. Liu's research from the 1970s and 1980s laid the technical foundation for the low-power digital signal processors that enable modern mobile phone calls, streaming video, and internet communications—technologies used by billions of people daily. Though largely unknown outside engineering circles, his contributions underpin much of the digital media infrastructure that defines contemporary life. 刘必治 1934 年出生于上海，后随家人迁往台湾，1954 年获得国立台湾大学电机工程学士学位，1956 年与其父亲 Henry Liu Sr.（同为电机工程师）一起获得布鲁克林理工学院（今纽约大学 Tandon 工程学院）电机工程硕士学位。四年后获得博士学位，此后长期在普林斯顿大学任教。

## [Terence Tao Launches Palomar: A Registry for Lean-Verified Mathematics](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/18/palomar-a-registry-of-lean-verified-mathematics/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Terence Tao announced Palomar, a new registry that catalogs GitHub repository snapshots containing Lean code that adheres to current best practices for formal mathematical proof. Named after the astronomical observatory, Palomar functions as an analogue of a preprint server but specifically for Lean-verified mathematical proofs, with a thorough but achievable submission process that Tao himself tested with his own recent formalization. This infrastructure development from one of the world's most prominent mathematicians could significantly accelerate the adoption of formal proof verification in mainstream mathematics by providing a centralized, curated discovery layer for Lean proofs. By making verified proofs more findable and citable, Palomar addresses a key gap in the formal verification ecosystem and may encourage more mathematicians to formalize their results. Palomar stores exact GitHub commit references rather than full repositories, which preserves reproducibility while avoiding duplication. Submission requires adherence to current Lean formalization best practices, and the system outsources identity and spam management to GitHub—reducing friction but also introducing a dependency on a single platform.

hackernews · matt_d · Aug 19, 02:41 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49355968)

**Background**: Lean is an open-source interactive theorem prover and functional programming language based on the calculus of inductive constructions, currently supported by the nonprofit Lean Focused Research Organization (FRO). Formal verification allows mathematicians to express proofs in machine-checkable code, eliminating the possibility of certain errors but requiring significant additional effort compared to traditional pen-and-paper proofs. Existing precedents for formal proof libraries include Isabelle's Archive of Formal Proofs (AFP), which has served a similar role for that ecosystem for decades, and the Metamath proof database for the Metamath community.

## [Marvell Unveils Memory-Disaggregation Portfolio for AI Bottlenecks](https://www.eetimes.com/marvell-targets-ai-bottlenecks-with-memory-disaggregation-portfolio/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Marvell has introduced a memory-disaggregation portfolio that combines SSD, CXL, and photonic fabric technologies to address AI memory bottlenecks by bringing data closer to compute resources. The approach targets the memory choke point that increasingly constrains large-model AI performance. As AI models continue to grow in size and computational intensity, the memory wall has become a critical constraint on performance, scalability, and energy efficiency. Marvell's integrated approach spanning storage, coherent interconnect, and optical fabric could reshape data-center architecture for hyperscale AI deployments, affecting cloud providers, GPU vendors, and enterprises running large models. The portfolio spans three distinct technology layers: SSD for high-capacity local storage, CXL for coherent memory expansion and pooling, and photonic fabric for chip-to-chip optical interconnect with substantially lower power per bit moved. Memory disaggregation enables elastic sharing of memory across nodes using high-speed interconnects like CXL and RDMA, breaking the traditional tight coupling between compute and DRAM.

rss · EE Times · Aug 18, 14:39

**Background**: Memory disaggregation is an architectural approach that decouples compute and memory resources, allowing them to be pooled and shared elastically across data-center nodes. CXL (Compute Express Link) is an open industry standard built on PCIe that enables cache-coherent memory sharing between CPUs, GPUs, and AI accelerators, allowing memory expansion beyond traditional DIMM slot limits. Photonic fabric technology, such as Celestial AI's, uses optical signals for chip-to-chip interconnect, potentially reducing data-movement power by over 80% compared to electrical traces.

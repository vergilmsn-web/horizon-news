## [Review of Centec ZX530S-24S2CQ Budget 10G/100G Switch](https://www.servethehome.com/centec-zx530s-24s2cq-review/) ⭐️ 6.5/10

ServeTheHome has reviewed the Centec ZX530S-24S2CQ, a 24-port SFP+ 10G switch equipped with two 100G SFP28 ports. The review highlights that the switch is powered by a Chinese Centec switch chip, making it a budget-friendly option for high-speed networking. This switch provides a cost-effective solution for network engineers who need 10G and 100G connectivity without the premium pricing typically associated with major Western switch vendors. It represents a significant entry for affordable, high-performance hardware in the data center market. The switch features 24 SFP+ ports for 10G connections and two SFP28 ports for 100G uplinks. Its competitive positioning is largely defined by the use of a Centec switch chip, which differentiates it from other solutions in its price tier.

rss · ServeTheHome · Nov 22, 18:00

**Background**: Traditionally, high-speed 10G and 100G switching hardware has been dominated by major Western manufacturers like Cisco, Juniper, and Arista, which come with high price tags. The emergence of cost-effective alternatives using chips from suppliers like Centec offers network operators a way to deploy high-speed infrastructure more affordably. SFP+ and SFP28 refer to the specific types of fiber optic module ports used for these data rates.

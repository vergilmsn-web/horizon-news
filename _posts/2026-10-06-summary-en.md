## [2026 Nobel Prize awarded to optogenetics pioneers Deisseroth, Hegemann, and Nagel](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=85537) ⭐️ 9.3/10

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for their groundbreaking discovery of channelrhodopsin and the subsequent development of optogenetics. This milestone validates a transformative technique that fundamentally changed neuroscience by enabling researchers to establish causal relationships between specific neural circuits and behavior in living brains. Channelrhodopsin is an algal protein that acts as a light-gated ion channel; when exposed to blue light, it opens to allow ions to flow into the cell, creating an electrical pulse.

rss · Solidot · Oct 5, 13:37

**Background**: Optogenetics is a neurobiology technique that combines genetic engineering with light to control specific cellular events within cells that express light-sensitiveion channels. For decades, neuroscience relied on indirect methods that could not prove which neural circuits actually caused specific behaviors or memories. By introducing genes encoding light-sensitive opsins into targeted neurons, scientists can now turn cells on or off with light, allowing for precise, causal investigation of the brain's functions in real-time.

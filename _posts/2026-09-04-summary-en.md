## [Samsung Leads NAND Market as Prices Surge 55% in Q2 2026](https://www.techpowerup.com/352305/samsung-leads-nand-market-as-prices-climb-55-in-a-single-quarter) ⭐️ 7.5/10

NAND flash contract prices surged 55% quarter-over-quarter in Q2 2026, driving a 70% QoQ expansion of the global NAND market, according to Counterpoint Research. Samsung retained the top position with a 28% revenue share, followed by SK hynix at 19% and Micron at 15%, while China's YMTC grew its share from 9% a year ago to 14%. This dramatic price increase signals a structural shift in the NAND market driven by AI infrastructure demand, with direct downstream effects on consumer SSD and device pricing. The continued reallocation of NAND capacity from consumer to enterprise channels means everyday hardware buyers will face higher storage costs, while smaller players like YMTC are gaining ground on the established leaders. The market growth is driven almost entirely by rising prices rather than higher shipment volumes, and TrendForce earlier estimated a 70–75% jump over the same period. Manufacturers are prioritizing high-margin enterprise and hyperscale orders for AI servers over retail drives, which has constrained consumer supply; most forecasts expect meaningful price stabilization only around 2027 since new NAND fab capacity takes years to build.

rss · TechPowerUp News · Sep 3, 17:08

**Background**: NAND flash is a type of non-volatile memory that retains data without power and is used in everything from smartphones and laptops to data center SSDs. Unlike DRAM, NAND stores data in floating-gate or charge-trap cells, with modern chips packing multiple bits per cell (MLC, TLC, QLC) to increase density. The NAND market has historically been cyclical, with prices swinging based on supply additions and demand from PCs, smartphones, and more recently data centers. The current cycle is unusual because AI-driven demand for high-capacity enterprise SSDs is pulling capacity away from the consumer market, creating a bifurcation in pricing dynamics.

## [Maskless EUV Lithography Emerges as Alternative to E-Beam](https://semiwiki.com/lithography/373583-maskless-euv-lithography-an-alternative-to-e-beam/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A recent article from SemiWiki introduces maskless extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography as a viable alternative to electron-beam (E-beam) lithography in semiconductor manufacturing. This new approach aims to leverage EUV technology to overcome specific limitations inherent in current electron beam systems. Developing maskless EUV lithography is significant because it could improve upon the throughput or resolution trade-offs of e-beam lithography, which is often limited by its serial writing mechanism. This could have substantial implications for advanced packaging and high-volume manufacturing where e-beam is currently bottlenecked. Conventional EUV lithography uses 13.5 nm light and a photomask, whereas maskless EUV would project the pattern without a mask, a technology noted as already highly complex. The article provides only an introduction, and specific technical challenges like focus and aberration control are not detailed in the provided text.

rss · SemiWiki · Oct 18, 15:00

**Background**: Lithography is the process of transferring circuit patterns onto silicon wafers, forming the foundation of semiconductor manufacturing. Electron-beam (e-beam) lithography is known for its extremely high resolution but suffers from low speeds because it writes patterns serially. EUV lithography uses a much faster, parallel projection method, traditionally requiring a physical mask, making maskless EUV an attractive hybrid concept for high-volume, high-resolution applications.

## [DRAM Revenue Jumps 59.5% QoQ in Q2 2026 as Supply Lags AI-Driven Demand](https://www.dramexchange.com/WeeklyResearch/Post/2/12825.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

TrendForce reported that DRAM industry revenue surged 59.5% quarter-over-quarter in Q2 2026 to nearly US$154.73 billion, driven by sharp increases in traditional DRAM contract prices as supply expansion continued to lag behind surging demand from AI servers, LLM training, inference workloads, and agentic AI applications fueling shipments of HBM3e, LPDDR5X, and high-capacity RDIMMs. A 59.5% QoQ revenue jump signals a severe supply-demand imbalance that will push memory prices higher across consumer electronics, servers, and GPUs, directly impacting AI infrastructure costs and the margins of device makers. This trend could constrain AI deployment pace as memory becomes a more expensive bottleneck alongside GPUs. The revenue figure of US$154.73 billion for a single quarter is notably large and may reflect either a reported typo or unusually strong pricing dynamics, as global DRAM market size has historically been much smaller. rss · DRAMeXchange (TrendForce) · Sep 7, 15:43

**Background**: DRAM (Dynamic Random-Access Memory) is the primary type of volatile memory used in computers, servers, and mobile devices. HBM3e (High Bandwidth Memory 3 Extended) is a stacked memory technology critical for AI GPUs, offering very high bandwidth through vertical die stacking. LPDDR5X is a low-power variant of DDR5 memory used in laptops, smartphones, and edge AI devices, offering data rates up to 8,533 Mbps with improved power efficiency. RDIMMs (Registered DIMMs) are server-grade memory modules with a register buffer that improves signal integrity and stability, enabling higher capacity configurations required by data centers running AI workloads.

## [SK hynix Accelerates 1c DRAM Node to Dominate Production Mix](https://www.techpowerup.com/352445/sk-hynix-boosts-leading-edge-1c-dram-production-to-a-dominant-position) ⭐️ 6.5/10

SK hynix is rapidly shifting DRAM production to its sixth-generation 10nm-class 1c node, which rose from 10% of output in Q1 2026 to a projected 35% by Q1 2027, surpassing the 1b node (33%) as the company's most dominant manufacturing process. This aggressive transition to the 1c node positions SK hynix to supply next-generation memory products—HBM4E, LPDDR6, and advanced DDR5—critical for AI infrastructure and high-performance computing, potentially strengthening its competitive lead over Samsung and Micron in the AI memory market. The 1c node features an actual design size of 11-12nm with 5-6 EUV patterning layers, compared to the 1b node's 12-13nm and ~4 EUV layers. SK hynix first demonstrated the 1c node with a 16Gb DDR5 chip in August 2024, and the node will produce HBM4E, LPDDR6, and DDR5, while the older 1b node continues to handle HBM3E, HBM4, DDR5, and LPDDR5X.

rss · TechPowerUp News · Sep 7, 17:22

**Background**: DRAM technology nodes differ fundamentally from logic process nodes; DRAM manufacturers use 10nm-class naming conventions (1a, 1b, 1c, etc.) that do not correspond directly to actual transistor dimensions. EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography) uses 13.5nm wavelength light to print extremely fine circuit patterns, and each new DRAM generation typically adds more EUV layers to enable further scaling. SK hynix's 1c node is the sixth-generation 10nm-class process and represents a key enabler for HBM4E and LPDDR6, which are essential for AI accelerators and next-generation mobile platforms.

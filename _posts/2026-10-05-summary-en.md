## [AWS Ends Data Center NDAs, Pledges $1B to Host Communities](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/amazon-ends-secret-data-center-pacts-and-pledges-usd1-billion-to-host-towns-aws-promises-30-000-home-efficiency-retrofits-amid-100-proposed-bans) ⭐️ 6.5/10

AWS has terminated its practice of using confidential agreements to hide data center details from local residents and pledged $1 billion over five years through its new “Built Together” program. This shift represents a major policy change in how hyperscalers manage public relations and local opposition as over 100 proposed bans on new data centers have emerged in the US. The $1 billion commitment covers education, energy, water, and local priorities, and the pledge includes 30,000 home efficiency retrofits to mitigate environmental impacts.

**Background**: Data centers hosting AI workloads consume massive amounts of electricity and water, causing noise and infrastructure strain on local communities. Previously, companies like Amazon used non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) to keep project details secret from residents, which sparked significant public backlash and local legislative pushes to ban new facilities.

A report analyzing PwC's Global Workforce Hopes and Fears Survey found that while 4 in 10 workers use AI tools weekly, specific barriers prevent a more widespread adoption rate. This data is critical for organizations seeking to understand why AI productivity gains are not uniformly distributed across their workforce, highlighting the gap between access and practical integration. The report specifically notes that despite the growing integration of AI into daily tasks, certain unspecified hurdles continue to restrict full utilization among the majority of the workforce.

**Background**: PwC's Global Workforce Hopes and Fears Survey is a comprehensive study that tracks employee sentiment, productivity, and technology adoption trends in the modern economy. As AI tools become increasingly prevalent, understanding the psychological and practical barriers to their use is a key challenge for human resource and organizational strategy.

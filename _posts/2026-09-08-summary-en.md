This is the first complete connectome of an adult animal at this level of complexity, representing a major neuroscience milestone that could transform understanding of brain function and inspire new AI architectures. The project combined electron microscopy with AI-powered image reconstruction over roughly a decade to trace every neuron and synaptic connection in the adult fly CNS. Google's earlier Hemibrain project mapped roughly half the fruit fly brain, but MaleCNS v1.0 is the first to cover the entire central nervous system including the ventral nerve cord.

## [High-NA EUV Lithography Transitions From R&D to Manufacturing](https://semiwiki.com/semiconductor-manufacturers/intel/373350-high-na-euv-moves-from-experiment-to-manufacturing/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Intel Foundry and ASML announced at the SPIE Photomask Technology and Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography conference that High-NA EUV lithography has transitioned from a development tool to a production technology, with Intel having processed wafers using the new system. This milestone is critical for advancing semiconductor manufacturing to sub-2nm process nodes, as High-NA EUV enables roughly three times more structures per area than current EUV systems and supports the industry's continued scaling roadmap beyond the limits of conventional EUV lithography. High-NA EUV uses a numerical aperture of 0.55 compared to 0.33 in current EUV systems, enabling optical resolution below 10nm. The transition to production addresses long-standing challenges in throughput, cost-per-wafer, and pellicle technology required for high-volume manufacturing.

rss · SemiWiki · Sep 8, 06:00

**Background**: EUV lithography uses 13.5nm extreme ultraviolet light generated from laser-pulsed tin plasma to pattern extremely fine features on silicon wafers, and ASML is the sole supplier of these systems globally. Numerical aperture (NA) measures a lens system's light-gathering ability, and increasing NA from 0.33 to 0.55 directly shrinks the smallest printable feature size. High-NA EUV tools are enormous and expensive—costing around $350 million each—which is why the transition to manufacturing readiness has been closely watched across the semiconductor industry.

## [ASML and TSMC to Lead Shift to 12-Inch Photomasks for High-NA EUV](https://www.electronicsweekly.com/news/business/asml-and-tsmc-to-lead-transition-to-12-inch-photomasks-for-high-na-euv-2026-09/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

ASML and TSMC have announced a collaborative initiative to lead the semiconductor industry's transition from 6-inch to 12-inch photomasks for High-NA EUV lithography. High-NA EUV will initially enter production using the current 6-inch masks, with the larger 12-inch format expected to follow to further increase productivity and lower chipmaking costs. This transition addresses a critical but often overlooked part of advanced semiconductor manufacturing infrastructure, since photomasks are the master plates carrying the circuit patterns projected onto silicon wafers. Larger photomasks remove stitching constraints, lower per-chip costs, and enable manufacturers to fully leverage High-NA EUV's resolution advantages for future leading-edge nodes. High-NA EUV lithography increases the numerical aperture from 0.33 in ASML's NXE systems to 0.55 in the newer EXE systems, enabling finer circuit features to be printed. The shift to 12-inch masks effectively quadruples the usable area per mask compared to 6-inch, allowing more die patterns to be exposed per plate and easing the stitching burden that comes with composing large chip designs from multiple mask fields.

rss · Electronics Weekly · Sep 8, 07:22

**Background**: Photolithography is the process used to pattern circuits onto silicon wafers, and photomasks (also called reticles) are the master plates that carry those circuit patterns. Standard EUV lithography uses 13.5 nm wavelength light with a numerical aperture of 0.33; High-NA EUV raises this to 0.55, allowing finer features to be resolved. ASML is the sole supplier of advanced EUV lithography scanners, while TSMC is the world's largest contract chipmaker and one of ASML's most important customers, making their joint move a de facto roadmap signal for the wider industry.

rss · Electronics Weekly · Sep 8, 05:14

**Background**: Josephson junctions are quantum-mechanical devices in which Cooper pairs tunnel through a thin barrier between two superconductors; they are the fundamental building blocks of superconducting digital logic, SQUID magnetometers, and qubit control/readout circuitry. Niobium-titanium-nitride (NbTiN) is a refractory superconducting material valued for its relatively high critical temperature, compatibility with thin-film deposition, and resilience in demanding cryogenic environments, making it attractive for scalable superconducting IC fabrication. Achieving high junction density through multi-level metallization is analogous to the back-end-of-line scaling pursued in CMOS, and is considered essential for moving superconducting electronics from small-scale demonstrations toward VLSI-scale systems.
